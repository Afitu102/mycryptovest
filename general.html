CFDS

A Contract for Difference (CFD) is a financial agreement that allows you to trade based on
an asset’s price movements without owning the underlying asset. CFDs often use leverage,
allowing you to open a position with a smaller deposit than its total value. While leverage
can increase potential gains, it also magnifies potential losses, and even small market move-
ments can significantly affect your trading account.

If the market moves against your position, you may lose some or all of your invested funds.
You are responsible for losses incurred in your trading account, subject to the applicable account
terms and protections under relevant laws.

Before trading CFDs on margin, carefully consider your financial goals, experience, and risk
tolerance. CFDs are traded over the counter rather than on an exchange and may carry greater
liquidity risks than exchange-traded products. CryptoVest sets CFD prices and trading conditions
in accordance with its applicable terms and order execution policies. CFD prices and features may
differ from the underlying assets, and the treatment of corporate events may differ from what inves-
tors receive when directly owning those assets. Review the relevant product details and risks before
trading.

CFDs are complex financial instruments that carry a high risk of rapid financial loss due to leverage.
Before trading CFDs with CryptoVest, ensure you understand how they work, the risks involved, and
whether you can afford potential losses. Any published statistic on retail investor losses should reflect
verified, current data specific to the provider.

CFDs are generally designed for short-term trading rather than long-term investing, as holding positions
overnight can incur additional fees. Sudden market movements, known as “gapping,” can also cause
prices to change sharply, particularly when markets reopen after closing, potentially resulting in less
favourable trading outcomes. Consider these risks and costs before opening or holding a CFD position
with CryptoVest.

While trading with CryptoVest, you must maintain sufficient funds to meet applicable margin requirements,
which can change over time. If the market moves against your positions or margin requirements increase,
you may need to deposit additional funds promptly. Failure to meet these requirements may result in the
closure of some or all open positions, and you may incur losses as a result.

Trading risks

Cryptocurrency markets are decentralised and may operate outside traditional financial regulatory frameworks.
Unlike government-issued currencies, cryptocurrencies are not controlled by a central bank, and their value can
fluctuate significantly. CryptoVest users should understand that cryptocurrency trading may not offer the same
investor compensation or dispute-resolution protections as regulated investment services. We are committed to
following applicable requirements for fair order execution and the responsible handling of client assets, where
required by law.

CryptoVest users should be aware that cryptocurrency services may not qualify for protections such as the
Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS) or the Financial Ombudsman Service available to eligible
customers of regulated financial providers. CryptoVest aims to apply appropriate practices for order execution
and client asset safekeeping in accordance with applicable laws and requirements.

Cryptocurrency prices are primarily influenced by supply and demand, market sentiment, and other market
factors. Prices can be highly volatile, difficult to predict, and vulnerable to speculation and market manipu-
lation. In extreme cases, a cryptocurrency may lose most or all of its value. Users should carefully consider these
risks before trading.

CryptoVest distinguishes between indicative prices shown on charts and actual trading prices available on the
platform. Since cryptocurrency markets operate across multiple exchanges, prices can vary between providers.
Therefore, chart prices are estimates and may differ from the actual prices at which trades can be executed on
CryptoVest.

Cryptocurrency transactions can present risks related to fraud, money laundering, and other illegal activities.
If authorities investigate suspected unlawful transactions, affected users may experience delays, restrictions,
or other legal consequences in accordance with applicable laws.

Cryptocurrencies are high-risk assets due to their volatility and uncertainty. Only invest funds you can afford to
lose, and carefully consider the risks before trading on CryptoVest.

Cryptocurrency trading is not suitable for everyone. Before using CryptoVest, ensure you understand how these assets
work, the risks involved, and whether trading aligns with your financial situation and experience. Cryptocurrency
trading carries a significant risk of loss and should not be considered a risk-free investment.

CryptoVest applies a spread to quoted market prices, where applicable. This spread affects the final trading price and
can increase trading costs, so users should review the applicable spreads before placing trades.

Cryptocurrency trading involves significant financial risk, and you should assess whether it suits your financial circum-
stances and risk tolerance. You could lose all the funds allocated to your trades. If market movements affect your
positions or margin requirements apply, you may need to deposit additional funds promptly. Failure to meet the applicable
requirements may result in your positions being closed at a loss, subject to CryptoVest’s terms and applicable laws.

CryptoVest supports cryptocurrency trading over weekends, but trading hours may change. Since cryptocurrency markets
operate continuously on many exchanges, prices may shift significantly over the weekend, potentially causing order delays

Under certain market conditions, you may be unable to close a position due to limited market liquidity or significant price
movements. Some cryptocurrencies may also carry additional risks depending on their features, market conditions, and

Newly issued cryptocurrencies can carry additional risks, including limited liquidity, difficulty selling after purchase, and

SPAC Risks

Investing in Special Purpose Acquisition Companies (SPACs) involves risks that differ from those
of traditional stocks. SPACs typically have no operating business when they go public, so their
success depends largely on management’s ability to complete a suitable acquisition. If a SPAC fails
to complete an acquisition within the required timeframe, it may be liquidated, and you could lose
some or all of your investment. Review the SPAC’s prospectus and other relevant disclosures carefully
before investing.
