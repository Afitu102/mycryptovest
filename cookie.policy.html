other relevant information.

  • Verify your identity to help protect your account and maintain security.
  • Maintain our website and ensure its features and functions operate properly.
  • Monitor and analyze how users interact with CryptoVest’s services to improve

  • Adjust our platform according to your trading habits and our regulatory requi-

WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small data files that websites store on your device to recognize returning
visitors and remember basic preferences. They are not executable programs and cannot
independently install malware. Cookie information may be updated during subsequent
visits to improve website functionality and user experience. For more information, visit
[All About Cookies](https://www.allaboutcookies.org/).

MANAGING COOKIES

At CryptoVest, we respect your privacy and provide options to manage the cookies used
by our website and services. Some cookies are essential for the platform to function pro-
perly and cannot be disabled while using these services. We also use certain third-party
cookies, and users can opt out of non-essential cookies where this option is available.

You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings. Refer to your browser’s
Help section or official website for instructions. Where permitted by applicable law, conti-
nuing to use CryptoVest’s web services with cookies enabled may indicate your consent to
their use.
