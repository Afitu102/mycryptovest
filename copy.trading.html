The Evolution of Copy Trading

Copy trading began gaining traction in the mid-2000s as online trading platforms
introduced ways to automatically replicate trading strategies. Instead of relying on
email signals or trading chat rooms, users could connect their accounts to a platform
and follow selected traders’ activities automatically. This innovation helped make
copy trading more accessible and contributed to its growing popularity across
financial markets.

For some investors, copy trading offers a more convenient way to explore market
strategies without making every trading decision manually. However, it
still requires careful research and an understanding of the risks involved.

How Trade Allocation Works on CryptoVest

CryptoVest’s copy trading feature calculates trade sizes based on the proportion
of the selected trader’s account allocated to a position and your allocation to that trader.

For example, if an expert opens a position using 10% of their account value and
you allocate 100% of your copy trading funds to that expert, the system may open a
corresponding position using approximately 10% of your allocated copy trading balance,
subject to platform settings and applicable trading limits.

Actual trade sizes and results may vary depending on account balances, available
funds, fees, and execution conditions. Copy trading involves risk, and losses are possible.

Maintaining Sufficient Funds for Copy Trading

To help CryptoVest execute copied trades, ensure your account has sufficient available
funds in the required currency and meets the exchange’s minimum order requirements.

For example, if the minimum order value is approximately $10 and a selected trader opens
a position worth 5% of their account, you may need a sufficient balance to meet that
minimum when the corresponding trade is calculated. The amount required depends on your
allocation settings, account balance, and the platform’s trading rules.

We recommend keeping an adequate portion of your allocated funds available to help reduce
missed trades due to insufficient balances. However, maintaining a particular balance does not
guarantee that every trade will be executed.

Automatic Trade Exits and Flexible Allocation

When a trader you follow closes a position, CryptoVest’s copy trading system can automat-
ically close your corresponding position according to your copy settings and execution
conditions. You can also adjust your allocation at any time to suit your preferences.
