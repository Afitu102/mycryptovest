What Is Forex Trading?

Forex, short for foreign exchange, is the global marketplace where currencies are bought
and sold. It is the world’s largest financial market by trading volume, with participants
exchanging currencies for business, travel, investment, and speculation.

Forex trading focuses on changes in exchange rates between currency pairs, such as EUR/USD
or GBP/USD. Traders aim to profit from price movements by buying or selling a currency pair
based on their market expectations. However, exchange rates can move unpredictably, and forex
trading involves the risk of financial loss.

Understanding Margin and Leverage

Margin is the amount of funds required in your trading account to open and maintain a leveraged
position. It depends on factors such as trade size, measured in lots, and the leverage available.
