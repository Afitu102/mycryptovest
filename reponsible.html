Responsible Trading

At CryptoVest, we encourage responsible trading across all platform features,
including social trading. Our Responsible Trading Policy promotes informed,
disciplined decision-making and helps users recognize the risks of emotional
trading. Beginners are especially encouraged to research carefully, avoid imp-
ulsive decisions, and understand the risks before placing trades.

To help users make informed trading decisions and avoid impulsive actions,
CryptoVest recommends the following responsible trading practices:

  • Maximum leverage:Up to 30:1 for major currency pairs, subject to
    applicable regulations and CryptoVest’s trading terms.
  • Maximum leverage: Up to 20:1 for non-major currency pairs, gold,
    and major stock indices, subject to applicable regulations and Cry-
    ptoVest’s trading terms.
  • Maximum leverage: Up to 10:1 for commodities other than gold and
    non-major stock indices, subject to applicable regulations and Cry-
    ptoVest’s trading terms.
  • Maximum leverage: Up to 5:1 for individual stocks and other refer-
    ence assets, subject to applicable regulations and CryptoVest’s trading terms.
  • Maximum leverage Up to 2:1 for cryptocurrencies, subject to applicable
    regulations and CryptoVest’s trading terms.
  • Limit exposure: Avoid allocating more than 20% of your account equity to
    a single trade. Responsible trading involves managing leverage, diversify
    ing your portfolio, and carefully assessing risk before making investment
    decisions.

Here are some practical tips to help you trade responsibly on CryptoVest:

  • Invest in What You Understand: Avoid making trading decisions
    based on rumours, random tips, or gut feelings. Before investing
    in an asset, research its history, price behaviour, and associated
    risks to make informed decisions.
  • Monitor Your Risk Score: If CryptoVest provides a Risk
    Score feature, use it to assess your trading behaviour and
    risk exposure. Aim to maintain a level of risk that aligns with
    your financial situation and risk tolerance.
  • Diversify Your Portfolio: Consider spreading your investments
    across different asset classes to manage risk. Choose trading
    options that match your experience, goals, and the level of
    monitoring you can comfortably maintain. Remember that
    diversification and copy trading do not eliminate the risk of
    losses.
  • Choose Traders Carefully: Before copying another trader on CryptoVest,
    review their risk profile, trading history, and portfolio diversification.
    Past performance does not guarantee future results, and copy trading carries
    the risk of losses.

Human Psychology & Emotional Trading

Traders of all experience levels can be influenced by emotions such as fear, greed,
and excitement, which may lead to poor decisions. Create a clear trading plan and
follow it consistently, even when markets fluctuate. When developing your plan,
consider the responsible trading tips above, paying particular attention to:

  • Maximum Leverage: Use leverage cautiously and stay within applicable limits,
    as higher leverage can magnify both potential gains and losses.
  • Portfolio Diversification: Consider spreading your investments across different
    asset classes to manage risk and reduce reliance on a single asset.
  • Other Traders’ Risk Profiles: Review other traders’ risk levels, trading history,
    and strategies before copying their trades. Remember that their performance can
    change and losses are possible.
